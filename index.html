💾 Backup & restore all data
Download one file holding everyone and every record (people, PINs, attendance, payroll, leave, lieu, overtime, term dates). Keep it safe. Restore reloads a backup over everything, which is how you bring the real names and records back after stripping them out of the code. Take a fresh backup before any big change or new version.
📅 Term dates
Anything outside these dates counts as a school holiday: no 08:55 sign-out, no 15:25 sign-in, and school children are in for full days.
🔑 Reset PIN to 0000

🔐 Manager & deputy PIN
These are NOT stored in the code, so they are not visible to anyone viewing the site. Reset puts the PIN back to its first-use default; whoever logs in next is made to choose a new one straight away. Manager default is 0000, deputy is 1111.
